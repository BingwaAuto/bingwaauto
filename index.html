How it works

Payment in. Bundle out.
Nothing in between.

Every step — from SMS detection to USSD confirmation — runs automatically while you focus on growing your bingwa sokoni business.

1

M-Pesa SMS arrives

Bingwa Auto intercepts the incoming payment notification in real time, even with the screen off.

2

Bundle matched

The payment amount is matched against your active catalog to identify the exact bundle to send.

3

USSD auto-dialled

The correct USSD code fires automatically. Interactive Tunukiwa *456# flows are stepped through too.

4

Logged & confirmed

Success or failure is detected from the USSD response and recorded in your transaction history.

Features

Built for bingwa sokoni agents,
not developers.

Everything you need to run a high-volume bingwa sokoni business — zero technical complexity. Install, grant permissions, subscribe, and you're live.

🤖

Full USSD Automation

Supports *180# standard codes and Tunukiwa *456# interactive flows — automatically steps through every menu. Works on dual-SIM devices with per-slot routing.

🛡️

USSD Signature Verification

Verifies the USSD menu structure before completing a purchase. If Safaricom changes a menu or the selected SIM shows an unexpected flow, automation stops before the wrong offer is purchased.

📲

Smart SMS Detection

Configurable sender filters and keywords ensure only legitimate payments trigger automation.

🔁

Retry & Scheduling

Failed jobs retry instantly or on an exact schedule you set — no manual re-runs needed.

📊

Full Transaction History

Filter by status — successful, failed, processing, paused. Tap any row for full details.

🔋

Always-On Service

Foreground service keeps automation running after app close. Auto-restarts on device reboot.

🔄

In-App Updates

New releases check automatically on launch. Download and install without leaving the app.
