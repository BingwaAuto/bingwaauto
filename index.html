How it works

Payment in. Bundle out.
Nothing in between.

Every step — from SMS detection to USSD confirmation — runs automatically while you focus on growing your bingwa sokoni business.

1

M-Pesa SMS arrives

Bingwa Auto intercepts the incoming payment notification in real time, even with the screen off.

2

Bundle matched

The payment amount is matched against your active catalog to identify the exact bundle to send.

3

USSD auto-dialled

The correct USSD code fires automatically. Interactive Tunukiwa *456# flows are stepped through too.

4

Logged & confirmed

Success or failure is detected from the USSD response and recorded in your transaction history.
