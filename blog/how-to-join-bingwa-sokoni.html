How to Join Bingwa Sokoni in Kenya (2026 Guide)

📅 Updated July 13, 2026 ⏱ 7 min read ✍️ By Bingwa Auto Team

Joining Bingwa Sokoni is one of the smartest moves for any Kenyan entrepreneur looking to earn extra income by selling Safaricom products. The registration process is actually very easy and takes only a few steps. In this guide, we'll walk you through exactly how to join Bingwa Sokoni and show you how Bingwa Auto can help you sell offers effortlessly.

What is Bingwa Sokoni?

Bingwa Sokoni is a Safaricom program that allows registered agents to sell bundles, airtime, and other digital products directly to customers. As a Bingwa agent, you earn commissions on every sale. It's a great opportunity, but the manual process can be time‑consuming. That's where Bingwa Auto comes in to automate the heavy lifting.

Requirements to Join Bingwa Sokoni

Before you start the Bingwa Sokoni registration, ensure you have these:

Safaricom SIM
Business Till Number
Notification SIM
Wait 30 days after receiving SIM

Step-by-Step Guide: How to Join Bingwa Sokoni

Follow these simple steps to complete your Bingwa registration:

📇
Step 1: Get a Business Till & Notification SIM

Obtain an M‑PESA Business Till Number from Safaricom. The Till comes with a dedicated notification SIM card that receives transaction alerts.

⏳
Step 2: Wait 30 Days

After receiving the notification SIM, wait for 30 days before proceeding. This is a mandatory Safaricom requirement.

📞
Step 3: Dial *180*5#

Insert the notification SIM into your phone and dial *180*5# on Safaricom network.

1️⃣
Step 4: Select Option 1 – Merchant or Agent

From the menu, choose Option 1 (Merchant or Agent).

✅
Step 5: Enter Your Till Number

Key in your Business Till Number when prompted. Your Bingwa Sokoni account is now active!

🎉 Congratulations – You're a Bingwa Agent!

Your Bingwa Sokoni registration is complete. You can now start recommending offers and earning commissions. But now comes the difficult part…

Why Selling Manually Becomes a Challenge

Many agents quickly realise that selling manually is exhausting. You have to:

  • Constantly check M‑PESA SMS for payments
  • Dial USSD codes repeatedly for each transaction
  • Risk sending the wrong bundle or airtime
  • Deal with slow customer service and network delays
  • Serve many customers at once – impossible to keep up
  • Manually record every sale – prone to errors

The good news? You don't have to do it all yourself.
